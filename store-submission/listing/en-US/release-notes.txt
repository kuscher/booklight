Units, time zones & pinned tasks.
• Convert units in the field: “72f in c”, “5 ft 10 in cm”, “16 oz in ml”, “65 mph in km/h”. Enter copies the answer; Copy number copies the digits; Pin keeps it on top.
• Time zones & world clock: “3pm sfo in berlin”, “14:00 cet”, “time in tokyo” across cities, zones and airports, with Pin and Add to calendar.
• Pin your checklist (“pin todo”) to keep open tasks from Todo.md on top, and use “tasks” to extract action items from copied notes into Todo.md.
