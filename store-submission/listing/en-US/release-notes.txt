First steps. At the very first opening Booklight says hello, then helps you give it a key: Action + Quick Insert is suggested, and the system's Keyboard shortcuts open with the steps on a page of Booklight's own. Three things to try, for practice: open an app, search inside one, a sum. One question before search suggestions are switched on. Type “first steps” to see it again, or choose First steps in the Booklight window. Updating without a key? You are shown the key's step once.
