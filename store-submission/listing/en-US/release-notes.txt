An event from a sentence. Type “Add dinner with Sam tomorrow at 7pm to the Team calendar”: one row shows what Booklight understood (when, where, the title, the calendar) and Enter opens Calendar, filled in. Allow Calendars in the Booklight window and a calendar is found by its name and completed as you type. Switch on “Save events without opening Calendar” and Enter saves what the row shows; a guess is never saved. Days and times are read anywhere in a line.
