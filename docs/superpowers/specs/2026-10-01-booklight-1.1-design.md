# Booklight 1.1: design

*1 October 2026. Follows Alex's answers of that day (§12). Builds on the 1.0 design
(`2026-10-01-booklight-design.md`), the interaction model (`docs/design/ux-model.md`), the design system
(`docs/design/design-system.md`), the permissions research and the device findings. Where this document and
one of those disagree, this one wins. The prototype is `docs/design/booklight-next.html`
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Nc98FHZosXidEaHqMpU1me).*

## 1. What 1.1 is

One release that carries what the plan called 1.1 Jot, 1.2 Dials, 1.3 Recipes and 2.2 Switches, on a new
foundation for acting on rows. Not in it: the Gmail relay (sending mail without the compose window), keep
awake (BentoBar has it), switching Do Not Disturb (a row opens the Modes panel instead), anything that
needs an accessibility service.

Success: every feature below works from the keyboard alone on a Googlebook; nothing pops into view; the
panel still opens in well under 150 ms and a keystroke still searches in well under 20 ms; Play asks for
no declaration form.

## 2. The interaction model

The panel stays one field over one list, row one selected.

**Actions.** The selected row shows *all* its actions as a row of icons at its right end, in a fixed order
per kind, at most eight. One is armed: it is unrolled to icon, name and the Enter mark, inside a small
pane of the panel's own veil. Enter runs it. Tab and Shift + Tab move the arming and wrap; Right at the end
of the text and Left do the same without wrapping (Left on the first action moves the caret again). Moving
to another row or typing re-arms the first action. A pointer arms on hover and runs on click. Destructive
actions are last, in error ink, never armed by default, never run by Ctrl + digit.

**Typed verbs.** A verb arms an action on the app's row; it never makes a second row. "chrome uninstall",
"chrome inf", "uninstall chrome". The whole text is matched as a name first: if any app matches it at
start-of-word quality or better, there is no verb reading ("play store", "open table"). A verb at the end
may be abbreviated from two letters (Uninstall: three); at the start it must be the whole word. Score = the
app's match × 0.95. English verbs also work in German. Only the row is learned from a pick, as in 1.0; a
typed verb arms its action again next time because it is parsed again.

**Scopes.** A scope is a row that takes text: a keyword search, a note, a timer. It is entered with Tab or
Enter on its row, or by typing its keyword and a Space at the start of the field. Its name and icon then
sit as a chip where the G was, the field holds only the argument, and the placeholder names the argument.
Backspace on an empty argument, or a click on the chip, turns the chip back into its keyword as text. The
last row in a scope is always "Search Google for" the keyword and the argument together, so an ordinary
word that happened to be a keyword is one Down + Enter away. Esc closes at every depth; Up on an empty
field brings back the last text that was not run, chip included.

**Arguments.** One line. Parts are separated by ` / `. The scope's first row is a preview with labelled
slots that fill as you type: typed values in full ink, guessed ones (no time given: "all day") dimmer,
empty ones a short rule.

**Controls.** A control is a row with a state (a level, or the three media keys). Left and Right act at
once and the panel stays open; they act when the caret is at the end of the text. Enter runs the armed
action (Mute, Done) and closes after 400 ms; Shift + Enter runs any action and stays. A control that needs
a grant is drawn hollow, says "Needs one switch in Settings, once", and its armed action is "Allow…".

**Tall rows.** 56 dp ordinary; 92 dp answers and previews; 208 dp a QR code; 256 dp a grid (emoji,
symbols). A grid is always row one; the four arrows move one square highlight; the footer names the cell.

**Confirmation.** For deleting something the user made, and "Forget everything": Enter arms, Enter again
(a new press at least 350 ms later) does. The pane turns to the error container, reads "Press again to
delete", and a line drains over 3 s. Any other key cancels; Esc cancels without closing. Uninstall has no
confirmation of ours: Android shows its own.

**Feedback.** After an action that leaves nothing on screen, the footer says what happened ("Copied",
"Added to Notes", "Timer started") for about half a second, then the panel closes.

## 3. What can be typed

Everything is a provider row or a scope. Keywords are localised; English ones always work.

| Typed | Result | Runs through |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `mail anna@x.com Lunch? / See you at 1` | preview To, Subject, Message. Compose · Copy text | `mailto:` (Gmail's compose window; nothing is sent) |
| `note buy milk / and coffee` | preview. Add · Keep · Copy | appended to `Notes.md` in the folder granted once; `CREATE_NOTE` |
| `event Fri 3pm Dentist @ Main St` | preview When, Title, Where. Create · Copy | calendar `INSERT` |

Ask Gemini is row one when the text has five or more words, ends in a question mark, or starts with a
question word and has three or more words, unless a local row matches at start-of-word quality. Otherwise,
from two words on, it is the row under "Search Google". With one word it is not shown.

## 4. The opening, the edge light, leaving

**Unfold** (the default; "Opening animation" in settings turns it off). The window is the panel's final
rectangle from the first frame and never changes width. Inside it:

1. 0 to 80 ms: a 6 dp seam of outline, centred on the field, grows up and down to the panel's height.
2. From 50 ms: the glass opens left and right out of the seam on a spring (damping 0.72, stiffness 1000).
   A value past the end turns back inwards ("lands"); nothing is ever drawn outside the window.
3. The contents are laid out once at full width, centred, and only uncovered; their alpha follows how far
   the glass is open (smoothstep 0.35 to 0.85). The blur radius stays 0 until the glass is half open, then
   rises to full; the dim follows the opening.

**Edge light.** Once the glass is open, one light runs once around the outline, clockwise from the top
left, in about 1.1 s, at constant speed along the perimeter, head in tertiary, tail in primary, on the
outline only. It also runs with the opening animation off.

**Off, or system animations off:** 1.0's arrival (scale 0.96 → 1, fade), or a cut.

**Leaving** is 1.0's: a 90 ms fade with a slight scale; the window is not touched.

## 5. The Booklight window and the app icon

The launcher activity stays the panel (the system's shortcuts start the launcher activity). When it is
started from the launcher, the taskbar or the Apps list (the caller is the home app, or the intent carries
source bounds), it starts the Booklight window instead and finishes without showing. Started any other way
(a keyboard shortcut, the assistant key, the widget, the tile, adb), it is the panel. The panel also no
longer closes for a focus loss in its first 600 ms unless focus is still gone 250 ms later.

The window is an ordinary resizable window on a solid ground, built from the panel's own parts: one
scrolling column 720 dp wide.

- **What it is.** The name, one sentence, and a stage on which the real panel composable plays a short
  loop (opens, "ch" typed, rows arrive, the selection moves, the arming glides, closes); a still when
  animations are off.
- **Your key.** How to bind one, the suggested keys as key caps, "Open Keyboard shortcuts".
- **Look.** Glass (Clear · Balanced · Frosted), Opening animation.
- **Search.** Engine, suggestions.
- **Your commands.** Quicklinks (1.0's keyword searches, now with `{argument}`, `{clipboard}`, `{date}`;
  `%s` still works; no placeholder = a plain link), snippets, recipes. Each row: Edit · Delete (second
  Enter). Add opens the editor: a small form in the window. A recipe is a name, a keyword and steps; a
  step is picked with a Booklight field inside the editor (an app, optionally in a place; a link; a
  settings page; a volume or brightness level; text to copy).
- **Results.** Settings pages, sums, Ask Gemini.
- **Notes.** The folder notes go to; Choose….
- **Access.** Brightness (Allow… opens the system page), the assistant role (opens the system's chooser).
- **Privacy.** What Booklight keeps; Forget everything; the privacy policy.
- **About.**

Choices are option strips with one gliding highlight; rows have the panel's metrics; sections rise in
22 ms apart, once. The first-run card under the field uses the same option strip, which replaces the two
buttons whose backgrounds and widths changed (the janky part of 1.0).

## 6. Other ways in

- **A search-pill widget** and **a Quick Settings tile**: both open the panel.
- **Text from other apps:** "Booklight" in the text-selection menu (`PROCESS_TEXT`) and in the share sheet
  for plain text. The panel opens with the text as a chip.
- **The assistant role:** the panel answers `ACTION_ASSIST`, so it can be chosen as the digital assistant
  in Settings. Never asked for; one row in the window explains it.

## 7. Access

| Permission | Why | What the user sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INTERNET` | suggestions, as 1.0 | nothing |
| `REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES` | Uninstall on app rows | nothing at install; Android's dialog per uninstall |
| `com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM` | timers, alarms, reminders through the Clock app | nothing |
| `WRITE_SETTINGS` | brightness | off until the user flips "Modify system settings" for Booklight |

No runtime permission, no accessibility service, no foreground service, no notification, no exact alarm.
The notes folder is a Storage Access Framework grant the user makes once. Play: no declaration form for
any of these; the data-safety answers do not change (nothing new leaves the device: mail, events, notes
to Keep and Gemini text are handed to the app the user sees open).

## 8. Architecture

The 1.0 split stays: `core/` is pure Kotlin with tests, `app/` draws and performs.

**core**
- `Model.kt`: `Query(raw, scope)`; `Action` gains `symbol`, `danger`, `confirm`, `done` (the feedback
  word); `Result` gains `armed` (which action a typed verb arms), `body` (`Body.Slots`, `Level`, `Media`,
  `Grid`, `Code`, `Mono`) and `nudge` (the two live effects of a control); `Icon` gains `Swatch` and
  `Glyph`; `Effect` gains the cases in §3's right column, `EnterScope`, `Steps` (a recipe) and `Grant`.
- `Scope.kt`: `interface Scope { key, keywords, name, symbol, hint; suspend fun rows(arg): List<Result> }`.
- `SearchEngine`: in a scope it returns that scope's rows in their own order plus the web fallback; outside
  one it ranks as 1.0 and adds rows that enter scopes. Up to two fallback rows keep the end (web, Gemini).
  `scopeFor(text)` finds a keyword and a space at the start.
- `Verbs.kt` (readings of a text as name + verb), `When.kt` (dates and times, English and German),
  `Jot.kt` (mail, event, reminder, timer, new), `Colors.kt`, `Templates.kt` (quicklink placeholders),
  `Clip.kt` (text transforms), `Secrets.kt`, `Emoji.kt` (index and search over a bundled table).

**app**
- `providers/`: apps (verbs, the new actions), sums, settings pages and panels, commands, web (addresses,
  ports, search, Gemini), dials (volume, brightness, media, Do Not Disturb), answers (colour, password,
  UUID), the user's own (quicklinks without an argument, recipes).
- `scopes/`: one small class per scope in §3, each turning a parsed argument into rows.
- `Executor.kt` performs every effect and stays the only place that starts activities, writes the
  clipboard or changes a system value; helpers for notes, files, audio and QR images sit beside it.
- `overlay/`: the activity (routing, focus, text intents), the model (scope, arming, confirmation, grid
  cell, last text), and the panel split into `Panel`, `Field`, `Rows`, `Strip`, `Bodies`, `Footer`,
  `Metrics`, `Glass`, `Motion`.
- `window/`: the Booklight window and the editor. `settings/SettingsActivity` goes.
- `entry/`: the widget, the tile, the folder picker.
- `data/`: `Settings` gains `opening`, `notesFolder`, `snippets`, `recipes`, `emojiRecent`, `showGemini`,
  `schema`; 1.0's `sites` become the quicklinks (a 1.0 file is migrated: `play` becomes `store`).

**Emoji data** is generated by `tools/emoji.py` from Unicode's `emoji-test.txt` and CLDR's English and
German annotations into `app/src/main/assets/emoji.tsv`; QR codes come from ZXing's core (Apache 2.0).

## 9. Motion rules that carry all of it

From the design system, as amended by the devices:

1. One highlight per level, and it travels: the list's pill between rows, the pane between actions, the
   square between grid cells. Never two, never a fade between two places.
2. The arming is one number on a spring. Each action shows as much of its name as the number is on it; the
   pane is drawn around wherever the number is; so name, pane and neighbours move as one.
3. Sizes are known before anything moves: names are measured once; the window's height follows one spring.
4. Text that mirrors typing changes in the same frame; objects spring.
5. Everything retargets from where it is; nothing restarts; with animations off everything cuts.
6. The window is exactly the panel, always (the blur is the window).

## 10. Testing

- **core:** JUnit for every parser and the engine's scope and fallback rules (target: the 40 tests of 1.0
  plus about 80).
- **device (Lenovo and HP):** `./bl debug` drives the panel; screen recordings stepped frame by frame for
  the opening, the arming, a scope entered and left, a level changed, the grid; over `./bl backdrop`.
- **by hand:** each row of §3 once, in English and German; light and dark; animations off; TalkBack on the
  action row.
- **reviews before release:** design (captures against the design system), code (correctness, privacy,
  failure paths), as for 1.0.

## 11. Risks

- **Window placement** (Left half, Right half, New window) depends on how each app and the desktop treat
  launch bounds; an app that is already running may just come forward. It ships if it works for ordinary
  apps on the device and says nothing false when it doesn't.
- **Space enters a scope** for ordinary words (`new`, `note`, `play`). Guards: the web row at the end, and
  Backspace.
- **The icon/shortcut routing** relies on the caller being the home app or the intent carrying source
  bounds; a wrong guess opens the panel, which is today's behaviour.
- **Size:** this is several releases in one. Order of building is foundation, Jot, Dials, Recipes,
  Switches, the window; each is committed working.

## 12. Decisions (Alex, 1 October)

1. Actions: in the row. "I don't like just seeing one action. I would like to see a row of actions each
   with an icon and maybe tab expands the selection to show text … make sure the animation is excellent."
2. Scope: Tab or Space. 3. Esc closes. 4. Controls live. 5. One line. 6. Uninstall: yes. 7. Only typed
   verbs teach an action. 8. Enter mark inside the armed chip. 9. Unfold. 10. Edge light once around the
   outline. 11. The icon opens the Booklight window. 12. Ask Gemini as described. 13. Notes.md in a folder
   granted once. 14. Clock alarm with a label. 15. Do Not Disturb: just open the Modes panel. 16. Keep
   awake: left to BentoBar. 17. `play` plays music, `store` searches the Play Store. 18. One big 1.1.
