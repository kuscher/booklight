Booklight 1.1: do things, not only find them.

- A row shows what it can do as a row of icons; Tab moves along them. "chrome uninstall" arms Uninstall.
- Jot: `mail …`, `note …`, `event …`, `remind …`, `timer 10m tea`, `new file …`. Ask Gemini for longer questions.
- Dials: `vol`, `brightness`, `pause`; an emoji grid; colours, QR codes, passwords.
- Your own links, snippets and recipes; `clip`; text from other apps; a widget and a tile.
- The panel unfolds when it opens. Theme, colours and transparency are yours to choose.
- The app's icon opens a Booklight window with everything in it.
