Booklight 3.2: an event from a sentence. Type an event the way you would say it, and Enter puts it in your
calendar: in the calendar's editor, filled in, or saved at once if you switch that on.

- **Type it the way you say it.** "Add dinner with Sam tomorrow at 7pm to the Team calendar": one row shows what
  Booklight understood (when, where, the title, the calendar), and Enter opens your calendar's editor, filled in,
  on that calendar. In any order, with the keyword `event` or without it: a line that begins with add or new
  event („trag … ein“, „neuer Termin“) and holds a day or a time, or with schedule, put, book or plan („plane“) and
  holds both. Such a row stands below anything of your device that matches and above the web.
- **Days and times are read anywhere in the line, and more of them**: `tmrw`, "the day after tomorrow", "on the
  12th at 6:45 in the morning", "7 in the evening", "from 9am to 10am", "Oct 14 to Oct 16", "all day"; in German
  „von 9 bis 10 Uhr“, „am 12.“, „vom 14. bis 16. Oktober“, „ganztägig“, „7 Uhr abends“. What leaves something open
  is shown as a guess, a step lighter: "at 7" (which seven?), "next Friday", "every Monday", a second day or time
  in the line. A year is shown wherever it is not this one.
- **Your calendars, if you allow it** (the Booklight window › Privacy › Calendars). Booklight then reads the list
  of your calendars, their names and colours, and never an event. Name one in the sentence, with "the" or without;
  its start is completed in grey and Right takes it; a Calendar stop on the row lists them.
- **The model on your device helps to split.** Where the rules cannot tell a place from the title, the model is
  asked a moment after you stop typing, and the words in the row change where they stand. It works out no date,
  nothing waits for it, and nothing it says is shown unless you typed every word of it.
- **Save without opening Calendar, if you switch it on** (the Booklight window › Results › Events). Enter then
  saves the event the row shows into the calendar you named, else the one you chose under "New events go to", else
  your account's own. "Open" is the second action. **A guess is never saved**: where the day, the time or the
  calendar is one, Enter opens Calendar and the footer says why. A line that begins with schedule, put, book or
  plan always opens Calendar: those are words of everyday searches too.

Two permissions are new, both the Calendar's, and neither is asked for until you press a row in the Booklight
window: reading the list of your calendars, and adding the event you save. Someone who allows nothing is asked
nothing: `event` works as before, with the better reading of days and times. Nothing in the background, no new
connection.
