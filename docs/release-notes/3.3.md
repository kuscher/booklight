Booklight 3.3: units, time zones and world clock in the field, a pinned checklist from `Todo.md`, and tasks extracted from text.

- **Units in the field.** Type `72f in c`, `5 ft 10 in cm`, `16 oz in ml`, `65 mph in km/h`, `15,5 gb in mb` or `36 h in d`: temperature, length (including feet and inches), weight (including pounds and ounces), volume and cooking measures, speed, area, data size and duration are answered as you type, in English and German. Enter copies the formatted value; the second action copies the plain number for a calculation or a table; Pin keeps it on top.
- **Time zones and world clock.** Type `3pm sfo in berlin`, `14:00 cet`, `15 uhr berlin nach sfo` or `time in tokyo` („uhrzeit in tokio“): times across time zones, cities and airports (`sfo`, `jfk`, `lhr`, `fra`, `hnd`, `nrt`, `sin`, `syd` and more) are answered on the spot, with the day change and offset difference. Enter copies; Pin keeps a live world clock or converted time on top; Add… opens your calendar at that exact moment.
- **Pin your open tasks (`pin todo`).** Type `pin todo` (or press Pin on any open task in `todo`) to keep the open tasks from `Todo.md` in the small window on top of your other windows. Open the window out and click a task (or press Enter) to tick it off in `Todo.md`; tasks added or ticked in the panel update the pinned checklist at once.
- **Tasks from text (`tasks`).** Copy meeting notes, an email or a checklist and type `tasks` (in German: `aufgaben`). Explicit task lines (`- [ ] …`, `TODO: …`) become task rows at once; unstructured prose is read by the model on your device and turned into individual rows where Enter adds a task to `Todo.md` while keeping the panel open so you can add several in turn.
- **Quality and hardening.** Bounded notes directory traversal, safer UTF-8 newline inspection before appending notes, and strict whole-word boundary matching when completing calendar names in `Sentence`.
