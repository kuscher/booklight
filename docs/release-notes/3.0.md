Booklight 3.0: do things inside your apps, watch a flight, and a highlight that moves like rubber.

- **One way to do things with an app.** Type the app, Tab to what you want, Enter; if it takes words, type them
  and press Enter again. An app's row is Open, Search, Play, Window and the arrow, and an app shows only what it
  has. Window opens the places for its window; the arrow opens App info, the app's pages in Settings and
  Uninstall. Typed words work as before (`chrome left`, `spotify notifications`).
- **Search inside an app.** `spo`, Tab, Enter, `daft punk`, Enter: Spotify opens on its results. The same for
  Netflix, the Play Store, YouTube and every app that can be searched. `yt lofi` and `store calculator` open in
  the app where it is installed.
- **Play a song by name.** `spo`, Tab, Tab, Enter, the song, Enter; or `play bohemian rhapsody`. The row shows
  what Spotify found and Enter plays exactly that: a song, an album, an artist, a playlist. Spotify by itself only
  searches, so this needs a free key of your own (the Booklight window › Labs); without it the row offers Search.
  "Which song is this?" asks the model on this device for a song you can only describe.
- **A flight you can watch.** With your own AirLabs key, a flight's row shows a line with the plane on it, where
  the flight is by the clock, the two airports and their times, and one badge: On time, Delayed, Landed late.
  Gate, terminal and the aircraft stand beside it when the service knows them. A pinned flight shows the same line.
- **More keywords.** `go hamburg hbf` and `go berlin to hamburg by train` for directions, `meet` for a meeting,
  `alarms` and `timers` for the Clock's lists, and `call`, `sms`, `wa`, `tg` where an app answers them. Booklight
  dials and sends nothing.
- **Your own commands for other apps.** A link may open an app (`spotify:search:{argument}`), and App commands
  under Commands › Yours ask an app for something: paste an `am start …` line or fill in the form, and Try it.
- **Labs**, a new section of the window, holds what needs a key of your own: Spotify and flights.
- **The highlight moves like rubber**: its front edge goes first, the old edge holds on for a moment and follows.
- **No typed letter is lost** when a keyword becomes a chip while you type quickly.
- **A new icon.**

No new permission. With a Spotify key in, the name you type after `play` goes to Spotify and nowhere else;
without a key nothing is sent.
