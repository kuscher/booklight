Booklight 2.1: letters your keyboard does not have, and sums with a decimal comma.

- **Letters.** `abc german` shows ä ö ü ß and their capitals, `abc danish` æ ø å, and so on for sixteen
  languages (French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Czech, Turkish, Swedish, Norwegian and more). Arrows
  move, Enter copies. Or ask by letter (`abc a`: ä æ å à â á ã …; `abc A` for capitals), by what it is written
  with (`abc ss`: ß, `abc ae`: ä æ) or by its mark (`abc umlaut`, `abc o slash`). Nothing typed: the ones you
  picked lately, then all of them. Typing `umlaut` or `accent` without the keyword finds it too.
- **A decimal comma.** `12*3,5` is 42. Where the system's language writes numbers with a comma (German, Danish,
  French …) answers are written that way too: `0,1 + 0,2` is 0,3 and `1.234,5 * 2` is 2469. The other sign
  still works where it can only mean one thing: `12*3.5` in German, `12*3,5` in English.
