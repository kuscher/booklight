Booklight 2.2: a calmer opening, glass that is frosted from its first frame, and a shorter row for apps.

- **The opening.** Medium speed is the default now (Settings has Fast and Slow), on a curve that starts slowly,
  is quick through the middle and lands over a long stretch, with no rebound. The seam the panel opens out of
  is seen again; since 2.0 it had been invisible.
- **Frosted from the first frame.** The blur used to arrive at the end of the opening, all at once. It now
  belongs to the glass: there from the first sliver, the same shape as the glass in every frame, and with it
  until the panel is gone.
- **A white reflection.** A few seconds after the panel has opened, in a quiet moment, one white light runs
  once round its edge, from the middle of the top. Any key and it fades. While the device's model is working
  the same light runs steady and dimmer.
- **An app's row has six icons:** Open, New window, App info, Maximise, Left half, Right half. The other
  places (thirds, quarters, centre) are in the list under the arrow.
- **The selection in dark theme is see-through,** like the panel: less colour, and the window behind still
  reads through it.
