Booklight 2.0: more to type, a window that stays, and answers on the device.

- **Prompts, answered on this device.** `fix teh text`, `shorter …`, `de …`, `sum`, `explain …`: the system's own
  model (Gemini Nano, where the device has it) writes the answer into the row. Enter copies. Nothing typed uses
  what you copied. Your own prompts in the window. Without the model, the Gemini app opens instead.
- **Pin.** A note, a sum's answer, a colour, a QR code or a countdown in a small window that stays on top and
  never takes the keyboard. `pin gate B22`, or the pin on a row; `timer 10m tea`, then Start and pin.
- **`?` lists everything**, each with an example. Enter on a row and Booklight types it for you.
- **Tips**: one thing Booklight can do, under the empty field, when you have opened it and not typed.
- **More places.** `chrome right third`, `files top left`, `code full`: thirds, two thirds, quarters, centre.
  The row shows nine actions as icons; the arrow opens the rest as a list under it.
- **`s` and `k`.** `s`, Tab: every settings page. `k`, Tab: what the system's keyboard shortcuts do
  (`snap` answers with the keys). Tab on any keyword makes it the chip.
- **Other apps' commands** as rows: `new event` opens the calendar's editor. A switch for all and one per app.
- **Notes that grow.** `notes milk` finds a line, `todo call the bank` adds a task and Enter ticks it,
  `note ideas …` writes to another file, Today's note keeps a file per day.
- **The Booklight window** has a column of sections: Start, Commands, Yours, Look, Results, Access, About.
- **A shadow around the panel** (off, low, medium, high), never under the glass.

Changed: the row of a one-letter keyword (`g`, `w`, `s`, `k`) now ranks under apps and settings, so `w`, Enter
opens the app, and `w`, Tab is Wikipedia. Store page is gone from an app's actions.

New in the manifest: Google's on-device AI library, which reaches the system's model. It adds two install-time
permissions without a prompt (binding to the system's AI service, and reading whether the network is up) and
reports how it is used to Google (device, app version, an installation id, sizes and timings; not your text).
