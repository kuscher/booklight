Booklight 2.3: what you just copied, a flight's times, your usual, and a window made for the desktop.

- **What you copied.** Copy something and open Booklight within two minutes: one line under the empty field
  says what it is ("Copied 20 s ago · a link and a date"). Tab opens it. Under it: the link (Open, or Copy
  clean without its tracking tail), the date as an event filled in, a phone number, a mail address, a flight;
  then your first three prompts, and Summary for a long text. A text that is not in your language has its
  translation first. Booklight reads the copy only when you press Tab; before that it knows only what kind of
  thing it is, and Android shows no "pasted" message.
- **Answers where they stand.** Enter on a prompt's row writes the answer into that row. Enter again copies it.
  Type a language to translate into it (`danish`), or say what to do with the text (`pull out the tasks as a
  list`). From the empty field: `tr danish see you on Saturday`.
- **Flights.** `LH455` is a row that names the airline; Enter opens the flight's page. With a free AirLabs key
  of your own (the Booklight window › Results › Flights) the row shows the route, when it leaves and lands,
  the status in words, the terminal and the gate. Pin it and a small window counts down to take-off, then to
  landing. Without a key nothing is sent.
- **Your usual.** Off until you switch it on (the Booklight window › Start › Your usual). With it on, the two
  things you run most and the one you ran last stand under the empty field. Nothing is selected: Down, then
  Enter. "Don't suggest" on a row takes it out.
- **The Booklight window is a desktop window.** Navigation at the window's edge, five sections (Start,
  Commands, Look, Results, Privacy), Material 3 Expressive rows and controls, every key, right-click menus, and
  a second pane on a wide window: a live preview of the panel beside Look, the editor beside your commands.
- **The model on the HP Googlebook answers.** The system calls it "downloadable" there although it has it;
  Booklight now tries it before handing over to Gemini.
- **What counts as a run:** opening a thing or doing its thing. App info, Edit, Delete and Uninstall no longer
  lift a row's rank.

No new permission. The flight lookups use the internet permission Booklight already has, and only with your
own key in.
